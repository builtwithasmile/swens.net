# swens.net — Direction (Josh's words)

**What this is:** the Porch. One quiet spot online that's actually mine — not a profile,
not a brand, not a storefront. A study with the door mostly closed. It's where I keep the
real version.

**Why it exists:** an identity anchor and a credibility hub — proof the things I've built
are real — not an autobiography. The open internet remembers everything and forgets nothing,
and most of my life doesn't need to be searchable. The people who should have the details
already have a key. Everyone else gets the vibe, and that's plenty.

**Two layers:**
- **Public** — just a feel: tone, a few thin links, nothing you could look me up by. No
  résumé, no pitch, no feed. It runs slow and says little.
- **Keyed** (behind the door) — the full ride: where I am now, what I'm building, the long
  version of the story, and a board to check in on. That's the whole social layer, on purpose.

**The business lives elsewhere.** swens.net only thin-links out to the ventures (Crossroads,
Selvatec, Maple, KillerBud) — it is not where they're sold.

**Rules that bind the public side** (from the 2026-07-10 round-table I ruled on):
- Achievement-shaped, never plot-shaped: what I've built and that it's real — never what
  happened to me, when, to whom, or for how much.
- Never publish exact age + exact location + real name together (a dossier, not a bio).
- No dollar figures, legal terms, or named clients on public pages.
- A confident single screen beats a long scroll — the format pulls toward memoir; don't.

**What it isn't:** a feed, a thing for sale, or something trying to grow. If it ever starts
feeling like work, I built it wrong.

— Swens
